# Orders: the same requirements, written without route names (demo)

Use these with `python cli.py link` to let a model propose the links.

- REQ-1 A signed-in customer can list their own orders.
- REQ-2 A signed-in customer can view an order, but only one of their own.
- REQ-3 Only a signed-in customer may delete an order.
- REQ-4 A customer's email must be unique.
- REQ-5 Only administrators may rebuild the search index.
- REQ-6 A customer can ask for a refund on an order.
- REQ-7 Orders can be exported as a CSV file.
- REQ-8 A customer's phone number must be unique.
- REQ-9 A signed-in customer can place a new order.
